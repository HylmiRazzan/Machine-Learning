This comprehensive dataset provides a longitudinal record of Jakarta’s Air Quality Index (AQI), known locally as Indeks Standar Pencemaran Udara (ISPU), spanning over fifteen years from January 2010 to February 2025. Sourced from the Satu Data Jakarta portal, the information is synthesized from five distinct monitoring stations across the DKI Jakarta province. To provide a definitive daily assessment, the dataset employs a "worst-case" representative model, where the highest AQI value recorded among the five stations is selected to characterize the city's overall air quality for that specific day.
